Why "Magic Susan"?

A lazy Susan that pays attention.

She started as a literal lazy Susan by our own front door, the spot where everything lands and nothing's ever found. A year of prototypes later, she earned the name: the quietly helpful one who keeps track so the household doesn't have to. She's real, she's in our homes today, and she's why we're here.

Questions

Frequently asked.

How Susan works, what VIP means, and everything in between.

So what is Magic Susan?

Susan is a secure wall organizer for your entryway with a quiet brain. Drop your keys, wallet, or badge in the tray and she recognizes each item on her own. From then on, the moment you head out without something you usually carry, she lets you know. One home for your essentials, one glance before you leave.

How does she know what to remind me, without nagging?

She only tracks what you've registered, and she knows what's in the tray versus what's gone. Take your badge? She stays quiet. Forget it? She speaks up. No false alarms, no reminders you don't need. Drop in something new and she'll ask if you want to register it; say nothing and she won't track it. You're always in control.

How do I set something up?

Drop it in the tray. She identifies what it is automatically: no typing, no scanning. Assign it to a person with a tap, pick your preferred nudge (voice at the door, phone notification, or on-screen note), and you're done. About thirty seconds. New items, edits, and removals are all handled from the app, anytime. Susan grows with your routine.

Does it work for the whole household?

Yes. Each person gets their own profile, their own essentials, and their own reminder style. Susan tailors the check to whoever is heading out: Emma's school list in the morning, Rob's gym list at 8:00. Up to five people, including the dog.

Is there a camera? What about privacy?

Yes, and it's designed to be the most unobtrusive camera you own. An internal one sees only the tray: not the room, not people. The optional front-facing camera for person recognition has a physical privacy cover and is off by default. The everyday item reminders work with no face recognition at all.

Is she really the world's first? Is it protected?

Magic Susan is the world's first smart entryway organizer: a new category, not a smarter version of something that already exists. The technology is patent-pending across several sensing designs, including the AI-vision version you're backing. Each is a distinct approach, protected separately, so the category and the methods behind it are ours to build on.
